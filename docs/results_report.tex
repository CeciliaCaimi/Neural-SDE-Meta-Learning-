\begin{abstract}
Meta-Diffusion adapts a frozen denoising diffusion probabilistic model to a new data domain
by fitting a low-dimensional task coordinate rather than by updating network weights. This
report gives the results of the current experimental stage, block by block against the
governing plan. The plan fixes the criterion in advance: the main claim holds only if the
correct source task together with a learned source-to-target relation outperforms
\emph{both} relation-only transfer \emph{and} target-only few-shot modelling. That criterion
is met at every target budget measured, but the margin over target-only modelling narrows
from $+0.0167$ at one target image to $+0.0044$ at twenty, so the method earns its keep
chiefly where target data are scarce. The architectural claim does not survive: against
matched modulation conditioning the additive basis is indistinguishable on that same metric,
and what remains is an efficiency result --- the same generation quality from fewer than half
the parameters. Seven of the ten deliverables are complete; three are not, and each is named
with its reason.
\end{abstract}

\section{Criterion and notation}

\paragraph{Criterion.} The main claim is supported only if the correct source task
\emph{plus} the learned source-to-target relation outperforms \emph{both} relation-only
transfer \emph{and} target-only few-shot modelling, in the low-target-data regime.

\paragraph{Notation.} A set encoder maps a set of images to a task coordinate
$z \in \mathbb{R}^{k}$. Writing $z_S$ for the coordinate encoded from source-domain data and
$c$ for a descriptor of the source-to-target relation, the transport map predicts the target
coordinate $\tilde{z}_T = z_S + \Delta_\gamma(z_S, c)$, taking no target image as input.
$M_S$ is the number of source images the encoder sees; $K_T$ is the number of target images
available at adaptation time. Feature-wise linear modulation, the standard alternative to an
additive basis, scales and shifts intermediate feature maps as a function of the coordinate;
it is called the modulation arm below.

\paragraph{Statistics.} Every comparison is a paired difference over episodes with a 95\%
confidence interval, and an asterisk marks an interval excluding zero.

\paragraph{The instruments.} Unless a section says otherwise, a distance is the sliced
Wasserstein distance, which the plan names as headline. Six measurements appear in this
report; they are set out here once because A2 uses all of them and they do not agree.

\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt \parskip0pt
\item \textbf{Sliced Wasserstein distance}, pixel space. Both sets of images are projected
onto many random directions; each projection is sorted and compared quantile by quantile,
and the result is the root mean square over directions. Raw pixels, nothing learned. Lower
is better.
\item \textbf{Transformation statistic.} Three fixed convolutions per image: mean absolute
Laplacian response, which is high-frequency energy; per-image standard deviation, which is
dynamic range; and mean absolute residual above a local box average. Blur and contrast
reduction both lower the first, only contrast lowers the second, so the triple separates
them. No training and no weights, so nothing in it drifts between runs. It was built for
CIFAR's corruptions and reads nothing outside them. Lower is better.
\item \textbf{Energy maximum mean discrepancy}, pixel space:
$2\,\mathbb{E}\|a-b\| - \mathbb{E}\|a-a'\| - \mathbb{E}\|b-b'\|$ over mean pairwise
Euclidean distances. It requires no kernel width, which is why it sits beside the
Wasserstein distance rather than a Gaussian-kernel discrepancy. Lower is better.
\item \textbf{Class consistency.} The share of an episode's generated samples that a trained
twenty-way classifier assigns to that episode's own class. \emph{Higher} is better. The
classifier reports its own held-out accuracy beside every verdict; in A2 that ceiling is
38.9\% on real images, so it is a weak instrument and is read as one.
\item \textbf{Superclass consistency.} The same at the level of CIFAR-100's twenty coarse
classes. \emph{Higher} is better.
\item \textbf{Kernel Inception distance.} The unbiased squared maximum mean discrepancy
under the cubic polynomial kernel $k(x,y) = (x\!\cdot\!y/d + 1)^{3}$, averaged over random
subsets. \textbf{The features are not Inception features}: this project has no InceptionV3,
so they come from the same small convolutional instrument quoted above. The estimator is
standard and the feature space is local, so the number compares the arms measured here and
cannot be set beside any published value. Lower is better.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Configuration.} One checkpoint of the three-transformation CIFAR-100 domain-shift
family --- Gaussian blur, additive Gaussian noise, contrast reduction at severity 3 --- a
U-Net backbone of 128 base channels, coordinate dimension $k=16$, 50{,}000 training steps
read with exponentially moving averaged weights. Adaptation uses 25 refinement steps at rate
$10^{-2}$. Generation uses the deterministic implicit sampler, 50 reverse steps, 96 samples
per cell, 60 episodes per cell.

\paragraph{Conclusion.} Both arms beat all three source controls at every budget, so the
source coordinate is worth having however the method is deployed. The criterion also
requires beating target-only modelling, and there the two arms part company at the largest
budget: \textbf{the method with refinement keeps a margin at every budget measured,
$+0.0167$, $+0.0138$ and $+0.0044$, each interval clear of zero}, while the unrefined arm
has given that margin up by twenty target images. The criterion is therefore met across the
range, but the margin at twenty target images is a quarter of the margin at one, so the
honest statement remains that \textbf{source information is worth most when target evidence
is scarce} and that its advantage narrows steadily as target evidence accumulates.

Against the source controls the refined arm's margins \emph{grow} with $K_T$ --- against the
mean source coordinate, from $+0.0286$ to $+0.0410$ --- which is what a procedure that
consumes target images should do against controls holding a coordinate that is simply wrong.
A gap to the oracle remains at every budget and for both arms.

\subsection{A2 --- Additive basis against the modulation arm}
\label{sec:a2}

The plan asks for the existing loss-level comparison to be extended to generation, matched on
backbone, latent dimension and budget.

Two networks were trained from scratch under identical conditions and evaluated on the
same 180 held-out episodes with one shared initial noise per episode. The backbones are the
same size --- 27.798M against 27.917M parameters --- and the arms share the set encoder, the
transport map, $k$, the base head and the loss. \textbf{Only the conditioning mechanism
differs.} In the basis arm the coordinate never enters the backbone: it weights learned
score-perturbation directions that are added to the output,
$\hat\varepsilon_z = \hat\varepsilon_0 + \sum_l z_l R_l(x_t,t)$. In the modulation arm it is
fed to the backbone and folded into every residual block's scale and shift. The parameters
that carry the coordinate are \textbf{55{,}344 in the basis arm against 118{,}784 in the
modulation arm}, so the modulation baseline is the larger of the two by more than a factor
of two; that is deliberate, since a stronger baseline is the one worth beating.

\paragraph{The oracle gap, and the explanation that failed.} The basis leads at the oracle on
the headline metric and on class consistency. The natural reading is that the basis offers a
loss surface the refinement can descend further, and that transport simply does not land
close enough to exploit it. A follow-up probe tested exactly that, from the same starting
coordinate on the same data under the same budget and noise draws, and \textbf{reversed it}:
the basis descends \emph{less} than the modulation arm from the transported coordinate
($-0.0024 \pm 0.0005^{*}$ as a fraction of each arm's own starting loss) and much less from
$z=0$ ($-0.0796 \pm 0.0121^{*}$), along a segment that is \emph{rougher}, not smoother
($+0.0002 \pm 0.0001^{*}$). The probe was written with its own reading rule: if neither
descent difference favours the basis, the oracle gap needs another explanation and must be
reported without this one. It does need another explanation, and none is offered here.

\paragraph{Conclusion.} \textbf{On the metric the plan names as headline, the additive
reverse-dynamics basis and matched modulation conditioning are indistinguishable on the
method's own arm} --- the interval contains zero at every target budget, with and without
refinement. Four of the five paired instruments place the modulation arm \emph{ahead} on that
arm, three of them significantly. The basis leads only at the oracle, and the mechanism
proposed for that lead was tested and contradicted. The single reading that favours the
basis on the transport arm is the kernel Inception distance, which is not paired and whose
feature space is local to this project.

What survives is an efficiency claim rather than a performance one, and it should be stated
as one: \textbf{the basis reaches the same generation quality as the modulation arm while
carrying the task coordinate in 55{,}344 parameters against 118{,}784, fewer than half.}
That is a real and reportable property of the architecture. It is not the claim the stage set
out to establish.

\paragraph{Conclusion.} The method beats reusing the source coordinate at every budget, the
advantage decaying monotonically by a factor of five and remaining clear of zero throughout.
This is the largest effect in the stage. Against target-only modelling the advantage is
confined to the smallest budget. \textbf{The plan asked for the crossing point to be named:
there is no crossing, only a convergence} --- the two become indistinguishable from two
target images onward, and the method was never shown to fall behind.

\paragraph{Refinement.} Refining the transported coordinate on the available target images
changes nothing that can be measured here: its gain over transport alone contains zero at
every budget, and so does the remaining gap to the oracle. \textbf{Refinement neither helps
nor hurts once transport has landed close.} Its value is real elsewhere in the same table
--- reusing the source coordinate improves from $1.439$ to $0.521$ across the sweep, and
that improvement is refinement doing the work --- which is the pattern to expect from a
procedure that closes distance: useful when the starting coordinate is far away, and
invisible when it is not. Per the plan, it therefore stays out of the headline method.

\section{Stage B --- Gaussian-mixture positive control}

data.} That is its purpose: the mixtures are analytically tractable, so the ground truth is
known and the mechanism can be checked against it without the instrument ambiguity that
attends any measurement on images. Nothing in this section is evidence about CIFAR-100, and
the one figure that shows both is labelled accordingly.

Five task families, 32 tasks each, one separately trained checkpoint per family. The
families are ordered by how far apart a task's source and target distributions are placed:
the four labelled by a perturbation size are increasingly distant, and the fifth pairs
source and target distributions drawn independently. This axis is the relatedness sweep,
included so that task distance can be varied deliberately rather than inferred.

\paragraph{Conclusion.} The four-coordinate scan orders itself exactly as the mechanism
predicts, in every family: the task's own coordinate is best, the shared mean and zero are
level with each other, and another task's coordinate is far worse than having none at all.
That last point is the informative one. Substituting the wrong task's coordinate costs
roughly $0.23$ where removing the coordinate entirely costs nothing, so the coordinate
carries task-specific content that actively misleads when it is wrong --- which is the
signature of a coordinate that is being used rather than absorbed. The profile is minimised
at the task's own coordinate in all five families.

Placed beside the same measurement on images, the well is $26.1\%$ deep on mixtures and
$1.6\%$ deep on CIFAR-100, both minimised at the task's own coordinate. \textbf{The
mechanism on images is neither absent nor misdirected; it is shallow.} Two observations are
unexplained: the relatedness sweep does not move the task-specific term at all although it
was built to, and on unrelated tasks the shared mean is \emph{worse} than zero
($0.6453$ against $0.6149$), the only family where that happens.

\section{Conclusion}

The criterion fixed before the stage began is met: the correct source task together with the
learned relation beats both relation-only transfer and target-only modelling at every target
budget measured. It is met narrowly at the top of that range --- the margin over target-only
modelling falls from $+0.0167$ at one target image to $+0.0044$ at twenty --- and the method
is therefore worth most where target data are scarce, which is the setting it was built for.
The positive control confirms the mechanism is sound where the ground
truth is known and locates the difficulty on images as shallowness rather than absence. Two
further results are usefully negative: source evidence saturates below the smallest budget
measured, so the method is cheap on the source side, and the non-linear transport map is not
distinguishable from a linear one, so the architecture can be simplified.

The architectural claim is the one that did not survive. Against a matched modulation
baseline carrying the coordinate in more than twice as many parameters, the additive basis
is indistinguishable at generation on the headline metric, and behind it on several
secondary ones; its only clear lead is at the oracle, and the loss-surface explanation
advanced for that lead was tested and contradicted. \textbf{What the basis can still claim is
efficiency: the same generation quality from 55{,}344 parameters against 118{,}784.} That is
worth reporting, and it is a different paper from the one the stage set out to write.

Two items would change what can be claimed. The unconstrained fine-tuning reference, which
is implemented but was not run, would establish whether the remaining gap to the oracle
belongs to the task or to the architecture. And the image release behind C0 is the only
route to a task family in which a single target example does not already reveal the task ---
which, on the evidence of A1, is where this method has the most to offer.
