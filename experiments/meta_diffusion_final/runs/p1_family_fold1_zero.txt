what this can and cannot support
  The full verified and cleaned archive supplies 2 held-out disease tasks.
  Paired intervals use one mean difference per disease. They are statistically valid
  for this split but necessarily imprecise with four test diseases; repeat-level noise
  is averaged within disease and never treated as independent evidence.
