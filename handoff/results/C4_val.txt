what this can and cannot support
  3 held-out tasks. Stage A had 60 episodes per K_T. A target-data
  equivalence statement of the form 'the method at K_T=2 matches target-only at
  K_T=10' needs a paired interval over held-out tasks and is not available at this
  scale; a directional reading is. Only two things move the three: leave-one-condition-out
  cross-validation (twelve training runs) or the authors' image release.
  The population contrast is I-III against IV-VI, forced by what is reachable: under
  I-II against V-VI the archive collapses to three conditions in total.
