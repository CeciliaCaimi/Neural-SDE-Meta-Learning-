Stage C model selection, on the three held-out validation conditions
==============================================================================
Selection criterion: validation loss_correct. delta_task and r_basis are printed
beside it and were NOT used to choose -- selecting on the quantity the experiment
is about would be circular. Where the curve is flat, the minimum is taken.

  run       ckpts   best val  at step  delta_task   r_basis  val at end  overfit
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  c_fitz       25     0.0628     2000    +0.00453     0.447      0.1527     2.4x
  cs128        16     0.0585     2500    +0.00259     0.503      0.1069     1.8x
  cs64         40     0.0571     3500    +0.00744     1.500      0.1063     1.9x
  cs32         60     0.0514     6000    +0.00518     2.075      0.0697     1.4x

  c_fitz   128 ch, 50 000 steps -- stage A's configuration
  cs128    128 ch, 8 000 steps
  cs64     64 ch, 20 000 steps
  cs32     32 ch, 30 000 steps -- also drops one level, attention and a block


what the sweep says
  Capacity was the whole problem. At 128 channels the validation loss doubles
  within a few thousand steps; at 32 it is flat over a budget seven times longer.
  The training side draws from 808 images behind five conditions, so stage A's
  50 000 steps is about two thousand epochs. The reachable archive does not fund a
  128-channel model; it funds a 32-channel one. Say that in the write-up rather
  than quietly reporting a different architecture from stage A's.

chosen: checkpoints/cs32_step6000.pt
  validation loss_correct 0.0514   delta_task +0.00518   gain_vs_zero +0.6136   r_basis 2.075
  task_specific_frac 0.8% -- read this the way the project's own note
  says to: gain_vs_zero is large because a 32-channel backbone cannot absorb the
  shared structure, so the basis carries it as a constant offset. The quantity
  that matters is the absolute delta_task, and at this checkpoint it is about
  three times the +0.00160 the matched CIFAR run reaches.

disclosure
  A C4 run on the discarded 50 000-step checkpoint touched the test conditions
  before this selection was made. Nothing from it informed the selection -- the
  checkpoint was chosen from the validation curve above -- and its numbers are not
  reported. It is recorded here because a test-split reading was spent.
