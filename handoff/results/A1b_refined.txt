THE CELL THAT MATTERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  against target-only at K_T=20:
    transport            -0.0028 +-0.0073   interval contains zero
    transport + refine   +0.0044 +-0.0031   interval excludes zero

  The method keeps a margin over target-only modelling at every budget measured.
  It is a quarter of the margin it holds at one target image, but it is there.
  Read on the unrefined arm the same cell says the advantage has gone, and that
  is the reading an earlier draft of the report carried.

  Against the three source controls the refined arm's margins grow with K_T
  rather than shrink, which is what a procedure that consumes target images
  should do against controls that are stuck with a wrong coordinate.
