3. VERDICT AGAINST THE PLAN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The retrieval itself succeeded: 99.5% of what is reachable is now on disk, and
  the 18 losses change no table. C0's projection was accurate to the unit -- it
  predicted 16 conditions above the floor and 8 able to fund C4, and the measured
  counts are 16 and 8.

  What the retrieval cannot fix is that the reachable archive is a quarter of the
  dataset. Against the plan:

    the plan's floor, N_S >= 30 and N_T >= 15      MET       16 conditions
    C4's support plus held-out query, N_T >= 45    NOT MET   8 conditions

  Eight fundable conditions allow at best two held-out test tasks. A paired
  interval over two tasks has one degree of freedom, which is not an interval.
  C4's headline result is a target-data equivalence statement -- the method at
  K_T = 2 matching target-only at K_T = 10, or similar -- and that form cannot be
  stated without a usable interval over held-out tasks.

  With the full dataset the same thresholds give 83 conditions above the floor and
  39 able to fund C4, roughly five times as many. The authors' image release is
  therefore the difference between a scaffold and an experiment, and it remains the
  one blocking item.

4. WHAT IS NOW USABLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  C1 and C2 can be built today on the 16 conditions: the domain module, the split
  builder, and the frozen evaluation predictors all need images, not a task split
  wide enough to report on. C3, the sanity check on abundant target evidence, is
  also reachable. Only C4 is blocked.
