3. CHOSEN GROUPING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  I-III vs IV-VI, chosen from the counts above, not in advance.
  Under I-II vs V-VI the Atlas-only table retains too few conditions to split into
  train/validation/test task sets at all, which C1 requires. The I-III vs IV-VI
  grouping keeps every labelled image and is the only one that survives the loss
  of DermaAmin. It is a weaker contrast in phenotype, and that must be stated in
  the write-up as a consequence of data availability rather than a design choice.

5. ROUTES TO THE IMAGES, RANKED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (a) Authors' request form, linked from the dataset README. Restores the full
      16,577 images. Must be submitted by the user; it is the only route that
      makes the 83-condition table above real. Blocking, and slow -- start it now.
  (b) Atlas Dermatologico direct. Works today, 3,905 images, no permission needed
      beyond the CC BY-NC-SA licence. This is the fallback the rest of C1-C4 can
      be built on while (a) is pending.
  (c) Internet Archive. The DermaAmin images are in the Wayback index -- a CDX
      query returns image/jpeg with status 200, snapshots from 2017. Coverage was
      not quantified: the CDX endpoint returned 503 and 504 under any bulk query.
      Retrieval would be a slow rate-limited crawl of ~12,600 files and should be
      treated as a repair for (a) failing, not as a plan.
  (d) Public mirrors: none usable. Hugging Face ZYXue/Fitzpatrick_17k is a 5,148
      row visual-question-answering derivative with no fitzpatrick_scale column;
      spycoder/fitzpatrick carries the annotations and a dangling 'path' column
      but no pixels; joshuachou/SkinCAP is gated. Checked 2026-09-16.

6. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR C1-C4
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Atlas-only gives 16 conditions above threshold. C1 splits the semantic tasks
  themselves into train/validation/test, so that is roughly a 10/3/3 split of
  conditions -- thin, but larger than the three transformations CIFAR ran on.
  With the authors' link it becomes 83 conditions, which is the version worth
  building the claim on.
  Neither dataset may be committed. Reference the image root through an
  environment variable read at import, as domains/cifar100.py does for
  CIFAR100_ROOT, and do not widen the repository root.
