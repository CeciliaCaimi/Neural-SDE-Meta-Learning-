The plan's stated expectation for A4 was improvement with increasing source
evidence, followed by saturation. What the sweep shows is saturation from the
first point measured: every movement across the whole range is smaller than the
half-width of the interval at either end. The marginal value of source evidence
is already exhausted below M_S = 16.
