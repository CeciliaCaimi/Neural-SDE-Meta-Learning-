how to read this
  seed CI     1.96 * SE over the four seed-level point estimates. This is the
              number to quote: it carries the seed-to-seed component that the
              within-seed half-width leaves out.
  within-h    the mean of the four within-seed half-widths, shown only so the two
              can be compared. Where 'spread' approaches it, a single run's
              interval was never the honest one.
  established same sign at all four seeds and the seed-level interval clear of zero
  suggestive  same sign at all four seeds but the seed-level interval contains zero
  ZERO        the seeds disagree on direction, so there is no effect to report
