the floor this has to clear
  'no adaptation' is z = 0, where both arms reduce exactly to the unconditioned
  U-Net: the basis term vanishes and FiLM's bias-free projections emit zero. Its
  cross-arm difference is therefore run-to-run variation of one architecture, not
  a conditioning effect, and a claim about the mechanism has to be larger than it.

caveats that travel with this table
  - one seed per arm. The four-seed sweep showed the transformation statistic's
    seed spread routinely exceeding its own within-run half-width, so a marginal
    row here is not settled until it is run again under other seeds.
  - validation split. The test split is untouched and is spent once, at the end.
  - the denoising-loss readings for both arms are in the training logs and are a
    supporting diagnostic only; this table is the conclusion.
