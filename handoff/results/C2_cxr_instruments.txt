  monotone across bins: YES
  AP - PA gap at equal true age runs from +5.4 to -6.2 years across the bins: the instrument reads AP films
  towards the middle of the age range. The confound on one set's reading is therefore about
  (AP share of the generated set - AP share of the real target set) x (the gap at the target bin).
  With AP-share differences up to 15 points that is at most 0.9 years, against 3.6 years between the closest
  pair of adjacent bin means -- bounded and small, and printed beside every age reading so a reader can apply the bound.
