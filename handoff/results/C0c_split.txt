run            2026-09-17, from the 3887 files retrieved in C0b

CORRECTION TO C0 AND C0b. Both counted a condition usable if its target side
could fund a K_T=20 support set plus a 25-image held-out query, and reported 8
such conditions. That test omits the source side. A condition also has to fund
M_S source images, and for a TRAINING task a source query batch on top, since
src_query feeds the source denoising term of the meta objective. Nothing at
meta-test reads src_query: evaluation touches src_support, tgt_support and
tgt_query only. So training tasks and held-out tasks have different requirements
and counting them alike gets both numbers wrong.

1. THE SOURCE SIDE IS THE BINDING CONSTRAINT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  M_S    train-capable   eval-capable      (source query batch 32 for training)
   64                2              6
   32                6              7
   16                6             12
    8                7             13

  At the M_S = 64 this project trains with, only two conditions can serve as
  training tasks. A4 measured that source evidence saturates below M_S = 16 --
  sixteen source images buy everything the transport map extracts from a source
  set -- and at M_S = 16 the counts become 6 trainable and 12 evaluable.
  A4 is therefore not only a deployment result; it is what makes Stage C
  constructible on the reachable archive at all.

All counts are of files on disk.

A held-out task needs  M_S source + 20 target reserve + 25 held-out target.
A training task needs  M_S + src_query source + 20 target reserve + a target query.

2. THE SPLIT THIS FORCES, AT M_S = 16
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Only six conditions can fund a training source query batch, so those six are the
  training tasks. The split is not a free choice on the train side.

  split    tasks   source imgs   support reserve   held-out target
  train        6           288               120               352
  val          3            48                60               208
  test         3            48                60               122
  total       12           384               240               682

  1306 images of the 1668 held by these twelve conditions are consumed; the rest
  are source images beyond M_S that no stream reads.

3. WHAT THIS PERMITS AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Three held-out test tasks is better than the one or two implied by C0 and C0b,
  and a paired interval over three tasks has two degrees of freedom. It is a usable
  interval, but a very wide one, and no amount of episode sampling narrows it:
  between-task variation is the term that matters and there are three tasks.

  The held-out target sets of the test tasks are 57, 34 and 31 images. CIFAR
  evaluates its distributional distance against a query batch of 128. A sliced
  Wasserstein distance computed against 31 real images is a much noisier instrument
  than anything in Stage A, and the comparison across the two stages should not be
  made without saying so.

  C4's headline form is a target-data equivalence statement. Three test tasks with
  thirty-odd held-out images each can support a directional claim; they cannot
  support an equivalence claim stated to a budget. With the full dataset the same
  thresholds give 39 conditions able to fund C4, and the arithmetic above stops
  being the constraint.
